Guide

CIJ, TIJ or laser?

Each technology suits different products and production lines. This is a general guide, and we'll confirm the best fit for your line.

CIJ

Continuous Inkjet

Prints without touching the product, using a fast stream of ink.

  • Suited to fast production lines
  • Codes bottles, cans, cartons and more
  • Uses ink, makeup fluid and solvent
TIJ

Thermal Inkjet

Prints sharp, clean codes from compact, easy-to-change cartridges.

  • Sharp text, barcodes and QR codes
  • Simple cartridge changes
  • Often used on cartons and cases
Laser

Laser Marking

Marks the surface itself, so there is no ink to run out or rub off.

  • Permanent, hard-wearing marks
  • No ink to buy or replace
  • Works on a range of materials

Common questions

What should I tell you to get a recommendation?

What you print on (bottles, cartons, sachets, cables), what the code should say, and roughly how fast your line runs. Photos of your product help too.

Do you supply spares and consumables?

Yes. We supply ink cartridges, makeup cartridges and solvent, along with spares for date coding machines.

Do you install and repair machines?

Yes. We handle sales, installation, servicing and repairs of CIJ, TIJ and laser date coding machines.

Which technology is right for me?

It depends on your product, surface and line speed. Use the guide above as a starting point, then talk to us and we'll recommend the best fit.
